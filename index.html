Phone microphones are not calibrated, so readings are estimates, typically within a few dB. To calibrate, play a known level next to a real meter and adjust the offset until they match. Fast uses a 125 ms time constant, Slow uses 1 s. Prolonged exposure above 85 dB(A) can damage hearing.
